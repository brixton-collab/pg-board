Every card is a hook you read word for word, three bullets on how to shoot it, and one line each on what makes it convert and what makes it travel — so you know what the shot is for while you're standing there. Nothing else. Four are golf, four aren't, which is John's rule, not mine.

The four that apply to every card
  • Frame one is never your face. It's the object, the number, or the other person.
  • Roll before you know the answer. If you already know how it ends, your face knows too.
  • Report it, never prescribe it. Last line is what you learned, past tense — never "you should."
  • One phone, no operator. Back camera for the thing, then flip to the front camera for the reaction.
